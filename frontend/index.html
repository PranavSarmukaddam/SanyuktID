Maharashtra Right to Public Services Act (महाराष्ट्र लोकसेवा हक्क अधिनियम)

The Government of Maharashtra is committed to delivering public services to citizens in a transparent, speedy, and time-bound manner under the Maharashtra Right to Public Services Act, 2015.

Through the Sanyukt ID system, all inter-departmental clearances are automated via standardized secure APIs. Citizens no longer need to physically visit multiple district offices to verify records or submit repeat documentation.

Citizen Rights:
  • Zero repetitive paperwork
  • Explicit data-sharing consent control
  • Real-time application progress tracking
  • Statutory delivery within declared SLA
